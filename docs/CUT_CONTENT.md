# CUT CONTENT - declared-unreachable-by-design (for the map contract suite)

> The map reachability contract (task #30) requires every SV cluster level to have an inbound
> entrance OR be listed here with justification. Anything here is intentionally not reachable and
> should NOT be flagged as a broken/unreachable-area defect.

> ⚠️ **"CUT" DOES NOT MEAN "NOT LOADED" (b89 lesson, 2026-07-27).** The engine streams levels by
> GRID PROXIMITY, not by reachability. A declared-cut level that sits inside a walkable grid block
> is still streamed in and still parsed. So a cut level's `0x0b` (RLTD navmesh) container must
> still be **structurally valid** - `ocean_extension05` crashed the game from inside a "permanently
> cut" declaration. Cut-ness therefore exempts a level from **MAP-NAV-3 only** (must-have-a-navmesh);
> `MAP-NAV-1/-4/-5/-6` deliberately ignore it. Never widen this list to silence a structural gate.

> 🧭 **THE LIST IS EXACT BASENAMES, NOT SUBSTRINGS** (BL-b89-DEBT-3, corrected 2026-07-28). The
> machine-readable copy is `tools/contracts/contracts_map.py:CUT_LEVELS`; the two must agree, and
> `_negtest_map.test_cut_levels()` asserts the live-vs-cut split. Its predecessor was a substring
> tuple that swallowed the whole `ocean_extension*` family; that over-broad exemption is exactly
> what let the malformed-container class hide for weeks.

## Genuinely cut

- **Cold Tombs** (`coldtombs`): SV shipped it with NO `0x0a` pathfinding geometry, so no navmesh can
  be generated by the standard pipeline, and no inbound binder exists in either SV or our merged
  map. ON HOLD per Will (task #36) - investigate whether it's a real dressed level (missing only its
  pathing layer → terrain-derived navmesh, a new capability) or an empty shell (→ content authoring,
  needs Will's design approval). Until then: intentionally cut. It DOES stream (it sits in Egypt's
  MiniDungeons grid) and therefore carries the b89 224-byte structurally-valid EMPTY container.
  **RETIREMENT PROTOCOL: this is a design HOLD, not a deletion - do not remove the level.**
- **Geometry-less ocean scenery tiles** - `ocean_extension05`, `ocean_extensionx01`,
  `ocean_extensionx03`, `ocean_extensionx04`, `ocean_extensionx05`, `ocean_extensionx06`,
  `ocean_extensionx07`. Backdrop only: 0 walkable tiles in all three tilesets and no other level's
  navmesh lists their GUID, so nothing rasterizes a cell they own. Each ships the b89 224-byte
  valid-EMPTY container (they stream in; `ocean_extension05` is the one that crashed Will).
