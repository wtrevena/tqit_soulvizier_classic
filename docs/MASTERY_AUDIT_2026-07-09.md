## 4) CROSS-CUTTING — systemic SV-port patterns + new build gates

- **A. CAST-ABORT CLASS (highest-value gate).** Three player nukes (Meteor/`MeteorShower`, Thunderball/`Ensnare`, Bonespire/`BoneSpire`) ship a `skillSpecialAnimationName` absent from the PC anim tables → `StartSkill` aborts (hard-law #2). **The detection logic already exists and is certified** in `tools/validate_soul_augments.py` — `_pc_universal_anims()` (builds the universal set from `anm_malepc01.dbr` + `anm_femalepc.dbr`) plus the abort check (~lines 171-175) — **but it is scoped to soul augments only, so the mastery trees were never checked.** → **NEW GATE `validate_player_skill_anims.py`:** reuse `_pc_universal_anims()`, walk every mastery tree (`malepc01.dbr skillTree1..12` → each tree's `skillName` slots + modifiers), assert each castable skill's `skillSpecialAnimationName` is empty or universal. Fail-loud, alongside the existing 3 DB invariants. **Would have caught all three.**
- **B. DEAD/DISABLED PET CONTENT.** `'xxx'`-prefixed skillNames, `records\drxplaceholder.dbr`, wrong-case dangling paths, `skillLevel=0` on a wired slot, omitted modifier slots — Spirit (bonefiend, Liche King) and Dream (MasterMind, timefield). → **Extend the dangling-ref sweep** to flag unresolved `skillName/petSkillName/buffSkillName` on shipped pets, `'xxx'` prefixes, and `skillLevel=0` on a slot whose target resolves.
- **C. `defensiveConvert == skillCooldownTime` COPY-PASTE ARTIFACT.** A negative cd-reduction ladder duplicated into `defensiveConvert`, silently lowering the player's charm/conversion resistance — **Nature (Accelerated Growth, Renewal) AND Defense (Resilience).** → **GATE:** flag any record where the `defensiveConvert` array equals the `skillCooldownTime` array.
- **D. DANGLING FX / SOUND / PROJECTILE / LOOT REFS (hard-law #4)** — widespread cosmetic across every tree (`SandBox\Chris\UnarmedProjectile_FX01`, `Skill_Lethal_Strike01`, leading-space PetFX path, Wildfire*Pak sounds, RingAll_01-08 loot). → **Full dangling-ref sweep across all mastery trees** (not just souls); also catches leading/trailing whitespace in paths.

OVER-TUNE WATCH (do not amplify further).** Isolated single-skill/pet items that already meet/beat Occult's best in a dimension: **Spirit Necrosis** (-100% enemy Vit/Bleed/Poison resist as a *permanent* aura, vs Occult/Hunting's windowed -60/-66% — mitigated only by 2.8-4.0 radius); **Nature treant** per-hit + 50%-current-life Stomp exceed Shadow Stalker (**this is precisely why Force of Nature uptime goes to 180 not 120**); **Storm Chain Lightning** group-petrify exceeds Occult's best AoE. # PART II - THE BENCHMARK (Occult/Hunting metrics + rubric)

## rubric

POWER-BENCHMARK RUBRIC - score any mastery on these 9 dimensions. Compare a candidate tree against the TREE-TOTAL budget of Occult (primary bar) and Hunting (secondary), NOT against a single skill. Every attack in these two trees is WEAPON-SCALED (adds %weapon-damage modifiers + flat pierce/bleed/poison), so express damage as two parts: (A) %weapon multiplier, (B) flat + DoT (weapon-independent). Values sampled at skill level L1 / skillMaxLevel / skillUltimateLevel (=gear-boosted ceiling).

1. SINGLE-TARGET DPS BUDGET. Best sustained ST engine (spammable attack: %wpn+flat / effective cooldown, net of energy) AND best burst nuke (highest single-hit %wpn+flat / its cooldown). Also count %current-life "execute" damage separately (ignores armor). Score both peak and sustained.
2. AoE / CLEAR BUDGET. For every skill hitting >1 target: targets x arc/radius x (%wpn+flat) per target. Sum simultaneous-target coverage. Distinguish true multi-hit weapon nukes from pure-CC AoE that deals little damage.
3. DoT & DAMAGE-AMPLIFICATION BUDGET. Flat DoT/sec at ult (poison+bleed+vitality, = flat/duration) PLUS the tree's OWN multipliers: %DoT modifiers, %weapon-ratio auras, and enemy resist-shred debuffs. This is where weapon-scaled trees hide most real damage - a tree with a -60% resist debuff + a +300% DoT modifier is far above its face damage.
4. DEFENSE / SUSTAIN BUDGET. Tree-intrinsic mitigation only (exclude gear): %dodge/deflect, +DA/OA, armor/absorb, elemental+CC resists, %life-leech, life-regen, and panic/immunity actives. Weight sustained mitigation over one-shot buttons.
5. PET BUDGET. Sum over pets of (max simultaneous count x [HP, DPS profile, CC-immunity, scaling]). Note whether the pets are one elite scaling summon or a disposable swarm, and whether pet damage is flat or difficulty-scaled. Include pet auras/self-buffs.
6. CC / LOCKDOWN DENSITY. Seconds x %chance of HARD CC (stun/freeze/petrify/confusion/fear) and SOFT CC (fumble/impaired-aim/attack-speed slow/run slow/root), and the area each covers. Count self-applied CC-immunity as defensive CC.
7. UTILITY / MOBILITY. Movement (blink, +run%), party auras (what stat, what radius/uptime), resist- and OA/DA-shred debuffs, projectile deflect/reflect, cooldown-reduction tools.
8. SCALING SLOPE INTO ULTIMATE. Two parts: (a) per-skill max->ult delta (%gain from the 4 gear-boosted levels); (b) mastery/tree "tail": are the % combat multipliers delivered by ML40 (practical cap) or gated behind deep ML41-72 investment most players never reach? A tree that FRONT-LOADS its multipliers is stronger in practice than these two even at equal face numbers.
9. ENERGY ECONOMY. Energy/cast of the core rotation vs. the mana pool + regen the tree itself provides. Classify: self-sufficient-cheap (rotation runs on tree's own resources) vs mana-hungry-needs-support (rotation requires the mastery's mana grant / external regen). Flag skills whose ultimate levels spike cost disproportionately.
(Optional 10 - SYNERGY COHERENCE: do modifiers, debuffs, auras and mastery stats funnel into one damage identity, or is budget scattered across unsupported paths.)

SCORING: for each dimension rate the candidate below-bar / at-bar / above-bar relative to Occult's tree-total, and note if any single skill/pet exceeds Occult's best in that dimension (a red flag for over-tuning).
